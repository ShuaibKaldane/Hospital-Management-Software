How does the free trial work?
Try MediCare+ for 7 days without a credit card or payment. You'll get admin access for up to 5 doctors and 50 patients. When you're ready to continue, choose the paid plan that fits your team. Start your free trial.
How does billing work?
Plans are billed monthly in Indian rupees, with {{ gstPercent }}% GST added at checkout. We accept UPI, net banking, debit and credit cards, and bank transfer. GST invoices are generated each month and emailed to your registered address.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. We prorate the difference when you change plans, so you only pay for what you've used. There are no lock-in contracts or penalties.
What happens after I purchase a plan?
You'll receive a downloadable file with your hospital's admin sign-in details. Use those credentials on the login page to access your hospital workspace and start setting up your team.
Can patients find my hospital right away?
Yes. Once your hospital is activated, it appears in the patient registration screen. Patients can select your hospital and start booking appointments the same day.
How is my hospital's patient data protected?
Patient data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Nightly backups and point-in-time recovery help protect your records. Your hospital's patient data is never shared with third parties.
