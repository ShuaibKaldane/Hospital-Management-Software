Frequently asked

Questions hospitals ask us.

What happens after I pay?

You'll instantly receive a downloadable text file containing your hospital's super-admin email and password. Sign in with those credentials at the login page and you're ready to go.

Can patients see my hospital right away?

Yes. The moment your hospital is activated, it appears in the patient registration screen for everyone using MediCare+ to sign up as a patient.

How does billing work in INR?

We bill monthly in Indian rupees. We accept UPI, net-banking, debit/credit cards and bank transfer. GST invoices are auto-generated every month.

Can I upgrade or downgrade later?

Yes — upgrade or downgrade from any plan at any time. We'll prorate the difference when you change plans.

Is my hospital's patient data safe?

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest. Backups run every night. We never share patient data with third parties.
